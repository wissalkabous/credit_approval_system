BANK FAQ KNOWLEDGE BASE

=== LOAN ELIGIBILITY ===

Q: What is the minimum credit score required for loan approval?
A: Applicants typically need a minimum credit score of 650 to qualify for a personal loan. For mortgage loans, a score of 700 or higher is generally recommended for the best interest rates. Scores between 580 and 649 may still be considered but often come with higher interest rates or require a co-signer.

Q: What is the minimum age to apply for a loan?
A: Applicants must be at least 18 years old to apply for any type of loan. Some loan products, such as mortgages, may require applicants to be at least 21 years old.

Q: What is the minimum income required to qualify for a personal loan?
A: Most banks require a minimum monthly income of 3,000 MAD (or local currency equivalent) for personal loans. This threshold may vary depending on the loan amount requested and the applicant's existing debt obligations.

Q: Can self-employed individuals apply for a loan?
A: Yes, self-employed individuals can apply for loans. However, they typically need to provide additional documentation, such as two years of tax returns, business bank statements, and proof of business registration.

Q: What is the debt-to-income ratio requirement?
A: Most banks prefer a debt-to-income ratio below 40%, meaning your total monthly debt payments (including the new loan) should not exceed 40% of your gross monthly income.

=== APPLICATION PROCESS ===

Q: What documents are required to apply for a personal loan?
A: You will typically need a valid national ID or passport, proof of income (pay slips or tax returns), bank statements from the last 3 months, and proof of residence (utility bill or rental agreement).

Q: How long does the loan approval process take?
A: The standard loan approval process takes between 3 to 5 business days after all required documents have been submitted. Pre-approval decisions for personal loans can sometimes be issued within 24 hours.

Q: Can I apply for a loan online?
A: Yes, most banks now offer online loan applications through their website or mobile app. You can upload required documents digitally, though some banks may require an in-person visit for final verification.

Q: What happens after I submit my loan application?
A: After submission, the bank reviews your credit history, verifies your documents, and runs a risk assessment based on your credit score, income, and existing debts. You will be notified of the decision via email, SMS, or phone call.

Q: Can I check the status of my loan application?
A: Yes, most banks provide an online portal or mobile app where you can track your application status in real time. You can also contact customer service with your application reference number.

=== CREDIT APPROVAL CRITERIA ===

Q: What factors determine whether my loan is approved or rejected?
A: The main factors include your credit score, income level, employment stability, existing debt obligations, and repayment history on previous loans. Banks use these factors to assess the risk of lending to you.

Q: Why was my loan application rejected?
A: Common reasons for rejection include a low credit score, insufficient income relative to the requested loan amount, high existing debt, incomplete documentation, or a poor repayment history on previous credit.

Q: Can I reapply after a rejected loan application?
A: Yes, you can reapply after addressing the reasons for rejection. It is recommended to wait at least 3 to 6 months, improve your credit score if possible, and ensure all required documents are complete before reapplying.

Q: Does applying for multiple loans at once affect my credit score?
A: Yes, multiple loan applications within a short period can negatively impact your credit score, as each application typically triggers a hard credit inquiry.

=== LOAN TERMS AND REPAYMENT ===

Q: What is the typical interest rate for a personal loan?
A: Interest rates for personal loans typically range from 6% to 15% annually, depending on the applicant's credit profile, loan amount, and repayment term.

Q: What repayment terms are available for personal loans?
A: Repayment terms usually range from 12 to 60 months. Longer terms result in lower monthly payments but higher total interest paid over the life of the loan.

Q: Are there penalties for early loan repayment?
A: Some banks charge an early repayment fee, typically 1% to 3% of the remaining loan balance. It is recommended to check your loan agreement for specific early repayment conditions.

Q: What happens if I miss a loan payment?
A: Missing a payment typically results in a late fee and may negatively affect your credit score. If payments are missed repeatedly, the bank may escalate the matter to collections or take legal action.

Q: Can I request a change to my repayment schedule?
A: Yes, in cases of financial hardship, you can contact your bank to request a restructured repayment plan, which may include extending the loan term or temporarily reducing monthly payments.

=== FEES AND CHARGES ===

Q: Are there any fees associated with applying for a loan?
A: Some banks charge a processing fee, typically 1% to 2% of the loan amount, deducted at disbursement. There may also be fees for late payments or early repayment.

Q: Is loan insurance mandatory?
A: Loan insurance (covering death, disability, or job loss) is often recommended and sometimes required for larger loan amounts, such as mortgages, but is not always mandatory for smaller personal loans.

=== GENERAL BANKING ===

Q: How do I open a bank account?
A: You can open a bank account by visiting a branch with a valid ID, proof of address, and an initial deposit, or through the bank's mobile app for basic account types.

Q: What is online banking?
A: Online banking allows customers to manage their accounts, transfer funds, pay bills, and apply for financial products through the bank's website or mobile application, without visiting a physical branch.

Q: How can I contact customer service?
A: Customer service can be reached via phone, email, live chat on the bank's website, or by visiting any branch in person during business hours.
